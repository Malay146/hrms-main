Models to Aggregate
This is the actual challenge behind the dashboard.
• Employees / Departments → headcount, ownership, grouping
• Contracts → wage, schedule, active employees
• Payruns / Payslips → salary totals, paid vs pending, trend data
• Attendance → presence, absences, late entries, overtime
• Time Off Requests / Allocations → leave taken and leave balances
Participant note — what this dashboard should prove
This dashboard is intentionally cross-functional. Participants should not populate it from a single model. | A good solution fetches and aggregates data from Payroll, Employee/Department, Contract, Attendance, and Time Off models. | The goal is to test how well they can combine business data into actionable HR/payroll insights.
Suggested dashboard requirements
Minimum dashboard content: | • Salary payment KPIs: total net salary, number of payslips, paid/pending state. | • Department overview: headcount or total salary by department. | • Time Off overview: approved leave days, pending requests, remaining balances by type. | • Attendance overview: present/absent/late counts, overtime or data-quality gaps. | • At least 2 visual summaries such as bar charts, line charts, stacked charts, or compact tables.
Problem statement note
Participants should understand that the Dashboard belongs inside the Payroll module, but its insights depend on multiple HR modules. | This creates a practical data challenge: define domains/filters, aggregate values correctly for the selected period, and present them in a way that is useful for payroll officers and HR managers.
Functional flow
Payroll → Dashboard → Apply filters (period/department/type) → View KPIs & charts → Drill down to Payruns, Payslips, Attendance, or Time Off records if needed.
